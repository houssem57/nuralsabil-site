القرآن والتلاوات والمصحف

يُنزَّل نص القرآن وترجمات معانيه مرة واحدة من Al Quran Cloud (api.alquran.cloud) ومن QuranEnc (quranenc.com). وتُنزَّل التلاوات آية آية من Islamic Network (cdn.islamic.network). وتستعمل صفحات المصحف خطوطًا تُنزَّل من Quran Foundation (verses.quran.foundation). ولا تحمل هذه الطلبات موقعًا ولا شيئًا عنك.

ما يكشفه كل اتصال بالإنترنت

كلما اتصل هاتفك بخادم، رأى ذلك الخادم عنوان IP الخاص بهاتفك؛ هكذا تعمل الإنترنت، ولا يستطيع التطبيق أن يمنع ذلك. ولا يرسل التطبيق اسمًا ولا حسابًا ولا معرّفًا للجهاز ولا معرّفًا إعلانيًا. ولكل خدمة مما سبق سياسة خصوصية خاصة بها.

ما يبقى على هاتفك

  • إعداداتك، وموقعك المحفوظ، وتقدّمك في الدروس.
  • القرآن الذي نُزِّل، والتلاوات، وصفحات المصحف، وقائمة المساجد القريبة.
  • تنبيهات الأذان والتذكير، وهي تُجدوَل على الهاتف نفسه؛ فلا يوجد خادم للتنبيهات.

لا يُرسل شيء من ذلك إلى أي مكان. ويمكن حذف ما نُزِّل من الإعدادات ← التخزين، ويُحذف كل شيء عند إزالة التطبيق.

ما لا يفعله التطبيق

  • لا حسابات ولا تسجيل دخول.
  • لا إعلانات ولا تحليلات ولا تقارير أعطال ولا تتبّع.
  • لا بيع للبيانات ولا مشاركة لها؛ فلا توجد بيانات تُباع أصلًا.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

In short: Nur al Sabil has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your exact location never leaves your phone. When the app needs information from the internet, it sends at most a position rounded to about 1 km, and nothing that identifies you.

Who is responsible

Houssem Farid
nuralsabil.app@gmail.com

Nur al Sabil is a free app made by one person. It earns nothing and contains no advertising.

Your location

The app asks for your location to calculate prayer times, to point to the qibla and to find mosques near you. Your exact location is used only on your phone and is stored only there.

For some features the app needs information from a service on the internet. In those cases it sends a position rounded to two decimal places (an area of about 1 km), never your exact position:

What forServiceWhat it receives
Prayer timesAladhan (api.aladhan.com)The rounded position and the month
The name of your townYour phone's own geocoding service (on most Android phones, Google)The rounded position
Nearby mosquesOur mosque files on GitHub Pages (houssem57.github.io)Only which 1° × 1° area you are in (about 100 km across)
Nearby mosques, where no file exists for your areaOpenStreetMap's Overpass serversThe rounded position and a search radius
The mosque mapOpenStreetMap map serversThe part of the map you look at

You can refuse location, or turn it off later in your phone's settings. The app then cannot show prayer times, the qibla or mosques for where you are; the lessons and the Quran keep working.

The Quran, recitations and the mushaf

The Quran text and its translations are downloaded once from Al Quran Cloud (api.alquran.cloud) and QuranEnc (quranenc.com). Recitations are downloaded verse by verse from the Islamic Network (cdn.islamic.network). The pages of the mushaf use fonts downloaded from Quran Foundation (verses.quran.foundation). These requests contain no location and nothing about you.

What every internet request reveals

Whenever your phone contacts a server, that server sees your phone's IP address; this is how the internet works and the app cannot prevent it. The app sends no name, no account, no device identifier and no advertising identifier. Each service above has its own privacy policy.

What stays on your phone

  • Your settings, your saved location and your lesson progress.
  • The downloaded Quran, recitations, mushaf pages and the list of nearby mosques.
  • The adhan and reminder notifications, which are scheduled on the phone itself; there is no notification server.

None of this is sent anywhere. Downloads can be deleted in Settings → Storage, and everything is removed when you uninstall the app.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of data. There is no data to sell.

Links

The About page links to the app's pages on Facebook, TikTok and WhatsApp and to Google Play, and the mosque map can open directions in Google Maps. These open only when you tap them, and from then on the privacy policy of that service applies.

Permissions

  • Location: prayer times, qibla and nearby mosques, as described above.
  • Notifications, alarms and start-up: to sound the adhan on time and to reschedule it after the phone restarts.
  • Internet: for the downloads and services listed above.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. Because the app keeps your data only on your own phone, you control it directly: uninstalling the app deletes it. For questions, write to the contact above. You also have the right to complain to a data-protection authority; in Hamburg this is Der Hamburgische Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If the app starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

Die App fragt nach deinem Standort, um Gebetszeiten zu berechnen, die Qibla anzuzeigen und Moscheen in deiner Nähe zu finden. Dein genauer Standort wird nur auf deinem Handy verwendet und nur dort gespeichert.

Für manche Funktionen braucht die App Daten von einem Dienst im Internet. Dann schickt sie eine auf zwei Nachkommastellen gerundete Position (ein Gebiet von etwa 1 km), nie deinen genauen Standort:

Diese Anfragen enthalten keinen Standort und nichts über dich.

Die App schickt keinen Namen, kein Konto, keine Gerätekennung und keine Werbe-ID. Jeder oben genannte Dienst hat eine eigene Datenschutzerklärung.

Was auf deinem Handy bleibt

  • Deine Einstellungen, dein gespeicherter Standort und dein Lernfortschritt.
  • Der geladene Koran, Rezitationen, Mushaf-Seiten und die Liste der Moscheen in der Nähe.
  • Die Adhan- und Erinnerungs-Benachrichtigungen, die auf dem Handy selbst geplant werden; es gibt keinen Benachrichtigungs-Server.

Nichts davon wird irgendwohin geschickt. Downloads lassen sich unter Einstellungen → Speicher löschen, und beim Deinstallieren der App wird alles entfernt.

Was die App nicht tut

  • Keine Konten und keine Anmeldung.
  • Keine Werbung, keine Analyse, keine Absturzberichte und kein Tracking.
  • Kein Verkauf und keine Weitergabe von Daten. Es gibt keine Daten zu verkaufen.
